Privacy Policy

Gamer’s Almanac
Last updated October 2026

The short version

Gamer’s Almanac keeps everything on your device. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive your notes, drawings, photos, play time or any other personal information.

What is stored, and where

  • Your games, notes, drawings, maps, pins, photos, play-time sessions and settings are stored only inside the app on your device.
  • The app also keeps an automatic copy in its folder in the Files app on your device, so you can recover from mistakes. It is protected while the device is locked.
  • Backup files exist only if you choose to save one. You decide where it goes. You can lock a backup with a password.
  • If you add a RAWG key, it is stored in the device’s secure Keychain, on this device only.

What leaves your device

  • Only if you add an optional RAWG key: the game name you type into the lookup, plus your key, is sent to RAWG (rawg.io) to find the game’s details, and the cover picture is downloaded from RAWG’s image server. RAWG’s own privacy policy applies to that request. Without a key, nothing is sent.
  • Links you tap, such as an image search for a cover or map, open in your web browser and are governed by that website.
  • Anything you choose to share through the share sheet, for example a backup file or a saved picture, goes where you send it.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you tap Take a photo, to add a picture to a page.
  • Photos: you pick pictures yourself with the system picker. The app does not browse your library.
  • Face ID or passcode: used only for the optional app lock. The system handles it, and the app never receives your biometric data.

Handwriting search

Searching your handwriting uses Apple’s text recognition on the device. Your drawings are not sent anywhere.

Widgets

The widget shows a game name and timer on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. Anyone who can see your screen can see it. The information is shared with the widget only on your device.

Deleting your data

Settings > Erase everything removes all games, notes, photos and the saved key from this device, and the automatic copies in Files. Deleting the app does the same. Backup files you saved yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page and in the app.
